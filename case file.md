## Additional items to check before closing the file

The following are especially important to verify, even where they are not maintained as separate documents or subfiles:

- [ ]Arrest and custody particulars: Ensure arrest dates, times, grounds and officer details are recorded in the appropriate records.
- [ ]Chain of custody: Ensure exhibits can be traced from recovery through storage, forensic examination and production in court.
- [ ]Disclosure: Identify relevant material that must be disclosed to the defence under applicable law, including potentially exculpatory material.
- [ ]Charge sheet accuracy: Check the offence, particulars, statutory provision, dates, names and court jurisdiction.
- [ ]Witness and exhibit consistency: Confirm that witness statements, exhibit lists, expert reports and the charge sheet are consistent.
- [ ]Missing evidence: Record outstanding reports, exhibits or investigations and take appropriate action.
- [ ]Authentication: Confirm that documents and electronic evidence meet applicable admissibility and authentication requirements.
- [ ]File completeness: Ensure the covering report, findings, recommendations and forwarding records are present where required.

Important: This is an expanded working checklist based on your subfile arrangement. It is not confirmation that every listed item is mandatory in every Kenyan criminal case. The exact requirements and the placement of particular documents should be checked against the current National Police Service and ODPP procedures, relevant laws, and the nature of the offence.
