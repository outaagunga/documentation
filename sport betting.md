
Absolutely. Let's make the formula **very simple**, so you can apply it to any SportPesa selection yourself.

## 1. Start with the basic idea

You need four numbers:

* **Odds** = the bookmaker's odds
* **Deposit tax** = 5%
* **Withholding tax** = 20%
* **Your estimated probability** = how likely you think the bet is to win

### Step 1 — Calculate your available stake

If you deposit **KSh 1,000**:

**Stake = Deposit × 95%**

So:

**1,000 × 0.95 = KSh 950**

---

## 2. Calculate your gross winnings

Formula:

**Gross payout = Stake × Odds**

Example: odds **1.80**

**950 × 1.80 = KSh 1,710**

Your profit before withholding tax is:

**1,710 − 950 = KSh 760**

---

## 3. Apply the 20% withholding tax

For a simple model where the 20% WHT is applied to the **betting profit**:

**WHT = Profit × 20%**

So:

**760 × 20% = KSh 152**

Your profit after WHT:

**760 − 152 = KSh 608**

Your final money:

**950 + 608 = KSh 1,558**

So your **KSh 1,000 deposit produces KSh 1,558** if the 1.80 bet wins, under this calculation.

---

# 4. The important formula

We can simplify the whole thing to:

### **Final payout = Deposit × 0.95 × [1 + 0.80 × (Odds − 1)]**

You don't have to use the complicated formula every time, though.

For practical betting, use this:

> **1. Deposit × 0.95 = actual stake**
>
> **2. Stake × odds = gross payout**
>
> **3. Gross profit = gross payout − stake**
>
> **4. WHT = gross profit × 20%**
>
> **5. Net profit = gross profit − WHT**

---

# 5. Now comes the REALLY important formula

This tells us **how often a bet needs to win just to break even after the taxes**.

### **Break-even probability = 1 ÷ [0.95 × (1 + 0.80 × (Odds − 1))]**

Let's test it with **1.80 odds**:

**Break-even probability ≈ 67.5%**

Meaning:

> At 1.80 odds, you need the bet to win **more than approximately 67.5% of the time** to have positive expected value under these assumptions.
